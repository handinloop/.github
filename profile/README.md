### Turn documents into structured data you can trust.

**HandInLoop** is an AI-powered document data-extraction platform for invoices, forms, receipts and other business documents. Models do the heavy lifting; a human-in-the-loop review step catches what matters — so the data you export is data you can rely on.

#### What we're building

- **Extraction** — pull fields and line items from invoices, receipts, forms and statements
- **Confidence-aware review** — low-confidence fields are routed to a person, not silently guessed
- **Clean outputs** — structured exports and APIs that plug into accounting and ops workflows
